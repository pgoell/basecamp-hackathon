# Master Services Agreement — Globex Manufacturing (customer paper, draft v3)

**1. Term.** Initial term of five (5) years. Customer may terminate for
convenience on thirty (30) days' notice with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

**2. Liability.** Supplier's liability under this Agreement is unlimited.

**3. Data.** Supplier may process Customer Data in any jurisdiction in which
Supplier or its subcontractors operate.

**4. Intellectual Property.** All deliverables, including pre-existing Supplier
tooling incorporated into deliverables, vest in Customer on creation.

**5. Audit.** Customer may audit Supplier's facilities and systems on 24
hours' notice, no more than twelve (12) times per year, at Supplier's cost.

**6. Payment.** Net 90 from receipt of a valid invoice.

**7. Governing law.** Laws of the State of Delaware.
